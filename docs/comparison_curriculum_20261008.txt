2026-10-08

The user selected stage validation for all four arms, with cumulative candidate
expansion only for native and native_listwise. selected keeps S:0..6 and
native_fixed keeps U:0..6. This is an explicitly versioned continuation of the
existing runs, not a claim that their earlier epochs used this policy.

The user challenged the initially proposed .95 MRR/.90 top1 gate as too strict.
The implemented policy uses stage patient-macro MRR >= .90 and top1 >= .85,
both satisfied in two consecutive completed stage validations, with at least
two completed training epochs in the same pool. Every active key must have
participated in a successful optimizer update for every training source.
These are declared operating thresholds, not empirically proven optimal values.

At adoption the current epoch finishes its original exact seven-key plan.
Its actual stage validation is diagnostic and does not count toward the gate.
The next epoch starts with the union of all historically successful candidate
exposures. Per-source exposure history remains intact. An eligible transition
adds the next seven unseen U keys (or the final remainder), retaining all old
keys. New keys receive the next epoch's first plan; subsequent plans continue a
checkpointed cyclic pool cursor. Every training forward still contains P+7.
No candidate or source is silently removed, and no forced expansion is used if
the threshold is not met. Reaching all128 within forty epochs is not guaranteed;
actual exposure and active-bank completeness are reported separately.
